# Editorial rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 2. A publishable result needs no zero and at least 13 of 16. Evidence integrity is blocking regardless of total score.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence | Unsupported or overstated | Traceable with ambiguity | Every material claim matches current evidence |
| Brief | Misses reader or action | Partly aligned | One reader, point, and action are clear |
| Voice | Generic or wrong persona | Some brand markers | Recognizable without imitation or caricature |
| Specificity | Abstract or padded | Mixed | Concrete where sources permit |
| Structure | Formula controls the piece | Some repeated shapes | Structure follows the argument |
| Rhythm | Uniform or performatively quirky | Readable | Natural variation suited to channel |
| Language | Jargon, filler, or ambiguity | Minor cleanup needed | Direct, precise, and reader-level |
| CTA | Missing, forced, or unauthorized | Relevant but generic | Natural and approved for the channel |

## Blocking failures

- invented or untraceable number, quote, result, price, customer story, or lived detail;
- stale or superseded offer information;
- persona or client-data leak;
- legal, medical, financial, or performance guarantee without authority;
- placeholder, unresolved attribution, or fabricated citation;
- CTA that performs an external action the owner did not approve.

## Batch checks

Compare all pieces together. Flag:

- two openings with the same grammatical mold;
- repeated examples or transitions;
- identical paragraph maps;
- the same closing device;
- claims that drift in wording or strength between pieces;
- keyword variation that changes the underlying fact.

Fix the weakest piece rather than adding random variation to all of them.
