# Profile and onboarding

Create a profile from evidence, not aspiration. Use interviews, approved copy, recorded language, offers, and customer-facing materials. Keep opinions about the brand separate from phrases the brand has actually used.

## Minimum viable profile

Complete these fields before judging voice:

- brand name and language;
- primary reader and their level of knowledge;
- position and promise;
- speaker perspective: company, founder, or named expert;
- three useful voice traits, each with a behavioral explanation;
- words the brand uses and avoids;
- forbidden topics and persona boundaries;
- proof and quote policy;
- CTA policy per channel;
- current source of truth for offers and prices.

Copy `assets/templates/brand-profile.example.json` into the client's working directory. Keep client profiles outside a public skill repository.

## Derive the voice

Collect three to ten accepted samples from the same speaker and language. For each trait, record observable behavior.

Weak: `confident`.

Useful: `States the recommendation in the first paragraph, names the tradeoff, and avoids guarantees.`

Do not turn a recurring mistake into a voice marker. Separate what the speaker naturally does from what readers benefit from.

## Approve the profile

Test it on two contrasting pieces, such as a practical article and a sales email. Ask the owner to mark:

- lines that sound recognizably right;
- lines they would never say;
- claims that require approval;
- missing vocabulary;
- channel-specific differences.

Update the profile, then freeze a version for the pilot. Record later changes deliberately so an agent does not silently drift the voice.
