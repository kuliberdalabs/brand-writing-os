# Examples

## Detail without fabrication

Source: `The owner reviewed the queue every Monday morning and reassigned overdue requests.`

Unsafe edit: `At 8:03 every Monday, she opened her laptop with cold coffee beside her and cleared the red alerts.`

Safe edit: `Every Monday morning, the owner reviewed the queue and reassigned overdue requests.`

The unsafe version sounds vivid but invents a time, object, action, and interface state.

## Claim strength

Source: `In a four-week pilot, the team reduced average manual triage time from 18 to 11 minutes.`

Unsafe: `The system cuts triage time by 40 percent.`

Safe: `During the four-week pilot, average manual triage time fell from 18 to 11 minutes.`

The safe version preserves the measurement, period, and limited scope.

## Voice without a formula

Generic: `In today's rapidly changing landscape, businesses must leverage AI to unlock efficiency.`

Edited: `Start with the queue your team clears by hand every morning. If the exceptions are predictable, that process may be worth automating.`

The edit names a decision and keeps uncertainty. It does not need a slogan, contrast trick, or inflated prediction.

## Honest missing evidence

Draft request: `Add that this saved the client 20 hours per month.`

Source pack: no time measurement.

Correct action: omit the number and return one question: `Do we have an approved measurement or client confirmation for the 20-hour claim?`
